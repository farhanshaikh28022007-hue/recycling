About Recycling

Recycling means collecting used materials such as paper, glass, metal and plastic, and turning them into new products instead of throwing them away. It matters because our planet's resources are limited and our landfills are filling up fast.

🌫️

Reduces pollution

Making products from recycled material uses less energy than starting from raw material, so fewer harmful gases and less water pollution.

🌳

Saves natural resources

Recycling paper saves trees, and recycling metal and glass reduces mining and quarrying.

🗑️

Reduces landfill waste

Less rubbish in landfills means less land used, fewer bad smells and less harmful leakage into soil and groundwater.

💼

Creates green jobs

Collecting, sorting and processing waste gives work to many people and supports local businesses.

Waste Segregation

Sorting waste at home is an important first step. Keep separate bins, keep recyclables clean and dry, and never mix wet food waste with dry waste. The bin colours below are examples; follow your local collection rules.
